3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect and store information about your interactions. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic Site functionality, such as session management and authentication via Supabase.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used by Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymized usage data about how visitors interact with the Site, helping us understand traffic patterns and improve content.
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to display personalized advertisements based on your browsing behavior across websites.

How Google Uses Data

When you use our Site, third-party vendors including Google use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more details, see how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Cookie Consent

When you first visit our Site, a cookie consent banner is displayed asking for your permission before any non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising) are loaded. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies. If you reject, only essential cookies required for basic site functionality will be used. You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your browser's local storage and revisiting the Site.

Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Site. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete existing cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all cookies

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:
