Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 29, 2026

At Korea Decode, accessible from https://koreadecode.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

When you visit Korea Decode, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns. This includes:

  • Your IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation patterns
  • Referring website or search terms that brought you to our site
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by contacting us via email).

Log Files

Korea Decode follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and the number of clicks. This information is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most websites, Korea Decode uses cookies. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. Cookies help us optimize the user experience and understand how our site is used.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our website. GA4 collects data such as:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on each page
  • Traffic sources (how you arrived at our site)
  • User engagement metrics (scrolls, clicks, searches)
  • Demographic and interest data (aggregated, non-personally identifiable)

Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information. You can learn more about how Google processes data at Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising Partners

Korea Decode works with the following advertising partners, each of which may use cookies and similar technologies to serve relevant advertisements:

  • Google AdSense: Google uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Trip.com Affiliate Program: We display travel booking advertisements from Trip.com. Trip.com may collect information about your interactions with their ads. Please refer to Trip.com's Privacy Policy for details.

These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their respective advertisements that appear on Korea Decode. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

Korea Decode's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information on their practices and for instructions about how to opt out of certain practices.

Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data that we hold.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.
  • Right to Opt Out of Sale (CCPA): If you are a California resident, you have the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. Korea Decode does not sell personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@koreadecode.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

Children's Information

Korea Decode does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best to promptly remove such information from our records.
